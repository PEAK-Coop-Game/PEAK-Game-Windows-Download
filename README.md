## 🧠 Overview

**PEAK** is a cooperative survival-climbing game that places players in the middle of extreme mountain expeditions where teamwork, endurance, and precise movement determine survival. Set across towering vertical landscapes filled with environmental dangers, the game challenges players to conquer difficult terrain while adapting to unpredictable natural conditions.

The core gameplay revolves around realistic climbing traversal, strategic coordination, and environmental awareness. Players must ascend steep rock faces, cross hazardous ridges, and navigate unstable surfaces while carefully managing stamina and positioning during dangerous climbs.

Every expedition introduces new obstacles and survival challenges. Sudden weather changes, collapsing terrain, narrow climbing routes, and risky traversal sections force teams to communicate constantly and make rapid decisions under pressure. One mistake can place the entire group in danger, increasing tension throughout each ascent.

PEAK emphasizes immersion through atmospheric world design, large-scale environments, and dynamic environmental effects that capture both the beauty and hostility of high-altitude exploration. The isolated mountain setting creates a strong sense of vulnerability while encouraging players to rely on teamwork to continue progressing upward.

The game also incorporates survival-focused mechanics that reward planning and adaptability. Players must conserve energy, coordinate movement, and support teammates during difficult climbs where timing and balance are essential for success.

With its combination of cooperative traversal, environmental survival, and physically demanding climbing systems, **PEAK** delivers an intense and rewarding mountain expedition experience for Windows players seeking challenging multiplayer adventures.
